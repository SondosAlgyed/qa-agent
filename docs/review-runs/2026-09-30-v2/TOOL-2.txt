Verdict: Ready with questions — the core registration, validation and age rules are implementable and verifiable, but several error paths and messages need confirmation.

1. **Missing required field behaviour** (Question, Gap): What should happen when one or more required fields are left empty — inline error per field, and with what wording?
2. **Duplicate email error undefined** (Question, Testability): What exact message and behaviour (inline on email field? generic message?) should a duplicate registration attempt produce?
3. **Validation message text** (Question, Testability): What are the exact inline messages for invalid email format and for a password shorter than 8 characters, so a tester can assert them?
4. **Age boundary and DOB validity** (Question, Ambiguity): Is a user whose 18th birthday is today allowed to register, and what happens for a future or non-existent date of birth?
5. **State after successful registration** (Question, Gap): Is the user signed in automatically, or redirected to the sign-in page, and is any confirmation shown?
6. **Password rules beyond length** (Question, Gap): Are there any other password constraints (maximum length, allowed characters, complexity) or is 8 characters the only rule?

Out of scope:

- Account lockout or brute-force protection on sign-in
